Geostoresgh landing page, round four

Three directions, each copied from a real store on Mobbin and re-skinned with the actual Geostoresgh catalogue (8 products, 4 categories, GH₵ prices, stock and ratings). Unlike the earlier rounds, every control on these pages works.

Option 1

Store

Copied from the Apple Store homepage

Two-tone headline, a rail of category and brand thumbnails that filter the grid, "The latest" large-card rail, calm product grid, help accordion. White, grey, hairlines; purple only on links and CTAs.

  • Most premium and calm
  • Best fit for a phones-first catalogue
  • Least promotional energy
Pick this if Geostoresgh should feel like an authorised, premium reseller.

Option 2

Department

Copied from Selfridges and adidas

Utility bar, centred wordmark, uppercase category nav, promo strip, split hero carousel with three real offers, "Recommended for you" rail, campaign tiles, New arrivals / Best sellers toggle, "Popular right now" list. Squared corners, black and white.

  • Most "big retailer" and confident
  • Carries promotions and price drops well
  • Densest page; most to maintain
Pick this if Geostoresgh should feel like a department store with deals.
Open Option 2
Option 3

Marketplace

Copied from Klarna and Faire

Search-first header, floating product thumbnails around a big search, dark category strip, then a real catalogue with a working filter sidebar (price, deals, brand, category), sort, chips and a mobile filter sheet. Soft pills, rounded tiles.

  • Most useful as the catalogue grows
  • Search and payment options front and centre
  • Friendliest; least "luxury"
Pick this if shoppers should find and filter fast and pay their way.
Open Option 3

What works on every option

  • Search with live suggestions (products and categories), keyboard navigation, and a real handoff to the store's search page
  • Add to bag from any card, quick view, or rail — with stock limits
  • Slide-out bag with quantity steppers, remove, subtotal, savings, and the GH₵ 1,000 free-delivery progress bar
  • Bag persists in localStorage across the three pages
  • Quick view modal with specs, rating, stock, quantity, and a link to the full product page
  • Saved items (heart) with a counter in the header
  • Category, brand, deals, and sort filters that re-render the grid
  • FAQ accordions, newsletter validation with success state, mobile menu
  • All links go to the real store routes on localhost:3000
How to view: serve this folder over HTTP (the pages load shared/ files), for example python3 -m http.server 8787 --directory design then open http://localhost:8787/landing-v4/. Resize the window to check mobile. Nothing under apps/ has been touched; once you pick one, it gets built as real components in apps/marketing.
