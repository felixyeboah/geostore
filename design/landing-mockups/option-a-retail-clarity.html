How do I pay?
Mobile money (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo), Visa and Mastercard are all accepted at checkout. Pay on delivery is available for orders inside Accra.
How long does delivery take?
Same or next day within Accra, and 2–4 working days to other regions. Your exact delivery cost is shown before you pay — never added afterwards.
What if the item isn’t right?
Unopened items can be returned within 7 days. Faulty items are covered by the stated warranty on the product page — typically 12 months.
Are the products genuine?
Yes. Every listing states condition, storage, and warranty length up front. If a detail isn’t on the page, ask us before you order and we’ll confirm it.
