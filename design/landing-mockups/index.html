GeoStore storefront — three directions

All three redesign the same page: apps/saas/app/(store)/page.tsx. Each uses the real catalogue — the 8 seeded products, 4 categories, actual GH₵ prices, ratings and stock counts — so you're comparing layout and tone, not placeholder text. Open each and scroll; they're all responsive, so resize to check mobile.

Option A

Retail Clarity

Light, calm and scannable. Split hero with a featured product, a four-up trust strip, category tiles, then a clean product grid.

  • Closest to your current brand
  • Purple used as accent, not wallpaper
  • Products visible fast, low visual noise
  • Fastest to build — reuses ProductCard nearly as-is
Pick this if you want it to look obviously more professional without a big rebuild or a change in personality.
Open Option A
Option B

Editorial Premium

Dark cinematic hero, serif display type, asymmetric category mosaic and a text-led product index. Reads like a considered boutique.

  • Highest perceived quality of the three
  • Leans on photography — needs good product shots
  • Slower, more deliberate browsing
  • Biggest departure from the current look
Pick this if you want GeoStore to feel like a premium curator rather than a general electronics shop.
Open Option B
Option C

Conversion Storefront

Bold and commercial. Promo ticker, split deal hero, countdown deals rail, filter chips and dense product cards with stock and savings.

  • Most aggressive at driving purchases
  • Price, savings and stock are the heroes
  • Familiar to anyone who shops Jumia or Amazon
  • Needs real discount & stock data to not feel fake
Pick this if conversion matters more than restraint and you'll actually run promotions.
