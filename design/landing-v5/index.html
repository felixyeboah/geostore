Geostoresgh · round five

Three clean directions,
built for one store.

All three take their cleanliness from SouqAfric — big confident headings against small product type, generous space, one accent — but each is a different visual language. Nothing here is a marketplace: no merchants, no vendors, one shop with eight real products.

Option A

Warm

Cream, ink and air

The closest to your reference. Warm cream bands alternating with white, a split hero with one tall product card, category tiles, a live product grid, ordering steps with small UI mocks, and a dark closing band.

  • Friendliest and most familiar
  • Carries offers and stock counts well
  • Warmest, least "tech"
Pick this if Geostoresgh should feel warm, trustworthy and easy.
Open Option A
Option B

Editorial

Paper, hairlines and big type

Quiet and typographic. Paper-white ground and hairline rules instead of cards. A split hero puts the statement and search beside one product plate, then a category index strip, a featured device, and the catalogue set like a printed index.

  • Most distinctive and premium
  • Fastest page, least decoration
  • Leans on copy and photography
Pick this if Geostoresgh should look like it has taste and nothing to prove.
Open Option B
Option C

Soft

Floating cards on grey

Modern and app-like. A floating pill header, white rounded cards on a soft grey ground, category chips that filter, a roomy product grid, and a purple closing card.

  • Most modern and product-forward
  • Scales best as the catalogue grows
  • Most conventional of the three
Pick this if Geostoresgh should feel current, quick and app-like.
