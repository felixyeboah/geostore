---
title: Privacy Policy
---

Geostoresgh collects account, contact, delivery, and order information that you submit. We also record the technical information needed to keep sessions secure and understand whether the store is working.

We use this information to provide accounts, process and fulfil orders, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, and improve the shopping experience.

Card and mobile money payments are processed by Reevit. We do not store payment credentials on Geostoresgh.

You can update account details and saved addresses from account settings. For access, correction, export, or deletion requests, email support@geostoresgh.com.
